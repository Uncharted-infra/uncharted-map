## Compass
- Allows users to see other people's travel plans to specific destinations, which can include the itinerary, hotels they are staying at, flights, restaurants & activities booked. 
## Passport

- Overview of the places they are traveling to, places they've been before, hotels, restaurants, activities they've done in the past as well - pretty much giving them an overview of the travel history, and plans to travel in the future. 

## Luggage - Places
- Places users saved for places they want to visit

## Luggage - Hotels
- Places users saved for hotels they want to visit

## Luggage - Flights
- Places users saved for flights they want to visit

## Documents - Itinerary
- The Itinerary that's planned for the user for the specific destination, whose going on the plan, hotel, budget, everything

## Documents - Receipts
- way to export all the expenses that have been carried out for the trip planning purposes, can export into a csv, xlsx, and pdf format. 

## Documents - Visas
- show valid visas they have for places they need internationally.
